01 ABOUT

Engineering at the intersection of intelligence and hardware.

I am an Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering student at IIEST Shibpur focused on the intersection of AI, computer architecture, VLSI, and efficient machine learning systems. My work spans RTL design, FPGA-based AI accelerators, hardware-software co-design, and hardware optimization.

I am particularly interested in how machine learning, computer architecture, VLSI, and intelligent EDA can come together to build scalable and energy-efficient computing systems.
